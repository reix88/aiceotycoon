Description

One-liner

Run an AI lab from your parents' garage to the Singularity — and decide whether your AI becomes humanity's best friend or its last mistake.

Short (≈50 words)

AI CEO Tycoon is a management game about building an artificial intelligence company. Start in a garage, train your first model, pitch investors, hire legends and race rival labs to AGI. But every shortcut has a price: the faster you grow, the less you can see what your AI is really becoming. Eleven endings await.

Long

You're a young founder with a laptop, a cat and a crazy idea. Your parents' garage becomes the birthplace of an AI company that will either give humanity a golden age — or end it. Train models generation by generation, from a clumsy chatbot to AGI. Raise money from investors who only care about growth. Move from the garage to a coworking space, a glass office tower, your own building and finally a campus — then data centers around the world and megaprojects in orbit. The world reacts to everything you do: news feeds explode, protesters gather under your windows, senators call you to hearings, rival labs ship faster and cut corners. And somewhere inside your servers, your AI is starting to notice things. Its awareness is hidden — you only see it if you invest in the tools to look.

Key features

  • From a garage to orbit — five headquarters, rooms you build and upgrade, a living office in cross-section.
  • Train your own AI — name it, release it, compare benchmarks with rival labs. Later it starts talking to you and remembers how you treated it.
  • The hidden danger — a secret awareness meter: safety costs time and money, ignorance costs everything.
  • Five rival labs — the hype-driven visionary, the corporate giant, the rocket billionaire, the safety purists and a state lab of a rival superpower.
  • Nearly 200 story events — board coups, Senate hearings, investor drama, your AI's first request.
  • Projects for Humanity — spend spare compute on protein folding, clean oceans or a cure for aging.
  • 11 endings — Golden Age, the Garden, Doomsday, "Not You", the Button and more.
  • Idle-friendly — real-time timers and offline progress: five minutes on the bus or a whole evening.
  • Legacy and sandbox — start a new epoch with perks, or keep playing forever.
  • The office cat — senses AI anomalies before humans do.
  • Satire, not a sermon — no real companies or people, just very familiar archetypes.
  • Lo-fi anime sunsets, rain on the garage roof and an original procedural soundtrack.

About the developer

Red Pixels is a one-person indie studio. AI CEO Tycoon was designed, coded, written and sound-designed by a single developer — the office cat supervised.

Frequently asked

Is it about OpenAI or another real company? No. The game uses fictional labs and archetypes. It's about the decisions every AI lab faces, not any specific one.

Is the game anti-AI? Neither pro nor anti. Good endings exist and are reachable — the outcome depends on choices: speed vs safety, money vs control, hype vs trust.

Monetization? Free to play with optional rewarded ads and purchases that save time or add cosmetics. No loot boxes, no pay-to-win endings.
